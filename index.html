Our story

How it all started

Mentoring gives answers. Coaching helps people find their own.

37+years of combined corporate experience
2ICF-credentialed coaches
BITS · IIMMTech & MBA foundations

Together, we bring more than 37 years of corporate experience, 17 and 20 years respectively, spent leading teams, building products and driving enterprise transformation. Our foundations were built at two of India's leading institutions: an MTech from BITS Pilani and an MBA from IIM Kozhikode, one grounded in engineering depth, the other in business and leadership. Our journey has moved with technology itself: from the early days of Java to today's world of AI transformation, AI governance and responsible AI.

But amid all the technology rush, something else kept catching our attention: the restless minds of the people around us.

Colleagues struggling to find balance between work and life. Talented professionals stuck at the edge of their next level. People wanting to change something within themselves but unsure where to begin. Others deeply unsatisfied with what they were doing, quietly longing for a career shift.

As managers and leaders, we did what we knew best: we mentored them. And mentoring helped. Over time we realised something deeper. When the answers come from within, they align the mind and the soul, and people truly commit to acting on what they have decided.

Yet the intention to coach was not enough. We wanted to do it right. So we learned the craft properly, trained through the International Coaching Federation, and became ICF-credentialed coaches.

That is how Souls Sarathi was born: from the belief that every person, at any stage, can pause, and rise again.

We don't hand you a map. We walk beside you while you take the reins.

Questions

Good to know

What is coaching, really?

Coaching is a thinking partnership. Instead of telling you what to do, we ask the questions that help you get clear on what you want, what is holding you back, and what you will do about it.

How is coaching different from mentoring or therapy?

A mentor shares their own experience and advice. Therapy often works through the past and emotional healing. Coaching focuses on your present and future: your goals, your choices and your next steps.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. As ICF-credentialed coaches, we follow the ICF Code of Ethics, which includes keeping what you share confidential.

How do I know which coaching is right for me?

You don't need to know before you start. Book a free discovery call and we'll explore it together.

How long does a coaching journey last?

It depends on your goals. We agree on the number and rhythm of sessions together during the discovery call.
